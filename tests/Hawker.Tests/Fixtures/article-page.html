Council approves new river bridge

By Jo Reporter

The city council has voted seven to two in favour of a new pedestrian and cycle bridge across the river, ending a decade-long debate over how to connect the two halves of the town centre.

The £14m structure will link the railway station with the market square. Supporters say it will cut journey times for thousands of commuters and bring new trade to shops on the eastern bank, which have struggled since the old ferry closed.

Opponents raised concerns about the cost and the effect on wildlife along the riverbank, but councillors were told that an environmental survey had found no protected species at the landing points.

“This is a once-in-a-generation investment,” said the council leader. “It will change how people move around the town for the next hundred years.”
